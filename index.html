Our Story

It all began with a simple belief: that the world moves a little too fast, and everyone deserves a beautiful place to slow down. Petal & Pour was born out of a love for two of life’s greatest simple pleasures—the quiet beauty of fresh blossoms and the comforting warmth of a perfect, handcrafted brew.A place where the aroma of freshly ground beans mingles with the subtle fragrance of seasonal petals, and where the seating invites you to stay just a little bit longer. Every corner of our space is curated to help you exhale. Whether you’re here to escape into a good book, catch up with an old friend, or simply find a moment of peace in your busy day, we’ve saved a cozy corner just for you.
